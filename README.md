### 👨‍💻 About Me

I am a **Full Stack & AI Engineer** and Computer Science undergraduate at **IILM University** (CGPA: 7.96) passionate about building high-availability web architectures, agentic AI workflows, and applied machine learning systems.

- 💼 **Full Stack & Service Engineer** at **[Hanku.ai](https://hanku.ai)**, developing and scaling LMS edtech infrastructure for global users.
- 🏛️ **Former Full Stack Developer** for **HSHEC (Govt. of Haryana)**, leading production deployments of educational governance portals on physical institutional servers.
- 💡 **President of Aignite**, leading student engineering chapters, hackathons, and technical initiatives under the Centre of Excellence in AI.
- 🛡️ **AI Security & Deep Learning Researcher**, actively developing **[PromptFence](https://github.com/Rudrakshsachdev/PromptFence)** (AI security testing lab) and **[DescriptAI](https://github.com/Rudrakshsachdev/DescriptAI)** (deep-learning e-commerce generation).
- 🏆 **Competitive Builder**: 4-time National Hackathon Winner across 10+ finals, Team Lead at **Paranox 2.0** (Top 40 of 1,500+ teams), and selected for the **BIRAC E-YUVA Fellowship** for *Q-HealthSecure* (Quantum-AI biomedical framework).
